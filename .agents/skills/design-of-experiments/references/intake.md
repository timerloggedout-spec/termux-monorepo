# Intake contract
Record objective, decision threshold, target population/process, estimand, response distribution and units, minimally meaningful effect, measurement resolution, repeatability, baseline variance evidence, manipulable factors, levels, nuisance covariates, experimental unit, treatment-assignment unit, observation unit, independent replicate, cost and constraints.

Ask: Can settings be independently assigned? What makes one unit independent of another? Are specimens nested within batches or subjects? Are settings hard to change? Is drift expected? Are combinations unsafe? What is the analysis model and which interactions matter? Is the purpose screening, estimation, optimization, comparison, or prediction?

Document exclusions and missing-data handling before collecting outcomes. Technical repeated readings improve measurement precision but do not create new independent treatment assignments. When information is missing, label assumptions and their consequences rather than inventing variance estimates or power.
