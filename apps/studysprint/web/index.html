New course

    
Create a course from its syllabus document (JSON with a
    course name, weeks and topics, and an exam_date if it has
    one). The course joins the switcher above; its plan and
    countdown follow the same core rules as the bundled courses,
    and its progress is stored under its own keys — never mixed
    with another course's.
