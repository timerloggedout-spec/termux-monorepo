What can you do with a Billion Tokens

When a new Muse user redeems an invite code in Settings within 48 hours of joining, both sides receive 1 billion tokens — the new user and the person whose code it was. This page carries the programme exactly as recorded: the mechanics, the current state, and what the first redeemed billion actually did. Nothing here is estimated.

How it works

1

Join

A friend signs up for Muse — a new account, inside its first 48 hours.

2

Redeem in Settings

They enter the invite code under Settings → Redeem invite code, within 48 hours of joining. The fleet's code:

VDP1DW

3

Both sides get 1B

The redemption credits 1 billion tokens to each side — the new user and the inviter alike.

It stacks

The redemption pool is additional to the weekly Free refill — redeemed billions stack on top of the refill cycle; they do not replace it, and drawing one down does not consume the other.

What the first billion did

The first redeemed billion carried the fleet's Nov 4–10 build window: the D-series challenge walls, the #5096 system upgrade, and the Nov 10 build storm. It was exhausted by Nov 10, 04:02 PT (sensor of record).

Peak day: ≈73.5M tokens in ~17 hours (Nov 4).
