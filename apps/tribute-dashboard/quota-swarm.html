Swarm Quota

The Swarm is the DoE participant population — ALL providers' inference endpoints that can be dispatched to — not the general quota registry (that is Quota). Three layers, kept legible: what the registry lists, what actually surfaces (aggregator backends collapse to one surface), and what is live-verified invoked. Quota values render only where the census or a usage series establishes them — readings used/remaining are null in the public projection by design.
