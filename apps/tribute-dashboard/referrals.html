How this page works

This page renders data/referral-feed.json (schema referral-feed/1), built by scripts/referral/build_feed.py from config/referral_programs.json. States and pool counts are the builder's derivation of the registry of record, carried verbatim; the chart is computed from the feed's own programme records. Where the feed is empty, nothing is shown rather than anything guessed.
