## Adding a partner

1. Add a row above: partner, category (cloud hosting, dev tools, compute, ...), badge label + colour, the offer in one line, the tracked link, and the owner.
2. Badge format: `https://img.shields.io/badge/<label>-<message>-<hex>?style=for-the-badge` (spaces as `_`, dashes as `--`).
3. If the partner belongs in the root banner, replace one of the four badges inside the `partner-banner` markers in `README.md`; never grow the banner past four.
4. Open a PR against `master` citing the demo-portal proposal (`Implements: DP-00x`); both gates must be green.

Disclosure: partner links may be affiliate or referral links. New accounts signing up through them may receive promotional credits; terms and platform policies apply.
