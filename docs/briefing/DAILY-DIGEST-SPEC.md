# Daily FOSS & Emerging Technology Digest Specification

## Objective

Produce a five-item morning briefing that acts as a compact research/procurement
radar for AI, science, FOSS, developer tooling, and emerging technologies.

## Standing priorities

1. breaking developments
2. deep / important research
3. practical tools and opportunities
4. major stories worth knowing
5. developments relevant to termux-monorepo
6. FOSS-driven research and procurement
7. horizon scanning using H0-H3 evidence lanes

## Standing resource inputs

Consult the resource families in config/foresight_digest_sources.json when
relevant. They are research inputs, not mandatory vendors. The daily search
should also discover new primary sources and new FOSS projects outside the
registry.

## Item contract

Every item must contain:

- concise headline
- horizon lane
- evidence status
- summary
- why it matters
- practical opportunity or procurement implication
- Termux-monorepo relevance when applicable
- primary/authoritative sources
- relevant image accompanying the headline
- confidence / unresolved questions when material

## Source discipline

Prefer primary repositories, official releases, technical documentation,
papers, standards, project maintainers, government/public research, and
first-party security advisories. Use secondary reporting for discovery or
context, not as a substitute when primary evidence exists.

Clearly separate:

- confirmed developments
- attributed claims
- research findings
- early signals
- speculation / scenarios

Never manufacture certainty from a weak signal.

## Termux-monorepo watch areas

Continuously scan for:

- MCP / agent protocols and tool interoperability
- OpenTelemetry and agent observability
- reproducible containers and environment parity
- complexity measurement and AST tooling
- experiment/evaluation infrastructure
- multi-agent orchestration and manager tournaments
- local / edge / ARM / Android inference
- FOSS model runtimes and open-weight ecosystems
- security of agentic workflows
- provenance, supply chain, and reproducibility
- compute, memory, energy, materials, and accelerator constraints
- research/procurement opportunities that reduce vendor lock-in

## Foresight lens

For each candidate ask:

- Is this a confirmed capability, emerging capability, weak signal, or scenario?
- What enabling technology makes it possible?
- What dependency or bottleneck could determine adoption?
- What FOSS project could provide an inspectable alternative?
- What would be worth prototyping now?
- What should merely be watched?
- What evidence would upgrade or downgrade confidence?

## Output discipline

Exactly five substantive items unless fewer than five genuinely significant items
exist. Do not pad the digest with low-value news. Include a short "Radar watch"
section only when there are material H2/H3 signals that do not warrant one of the
five main items.
