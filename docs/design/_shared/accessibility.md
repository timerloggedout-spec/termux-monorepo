## Design record requirements

Each system's `design.md` records evidence for:
- keyboard operation and visible focus;
- screen-reader names, roles, and state;
- contrast and non-color status cues;
- responsive behavior and text scaling;
- reduced-motion behavior where motion exists;
- terminal-specific navigation and constrained display behavior.

## Evidence discipline

A linked guidance document establishes a reference, not proof that every product complies. Record implementation and test evidence per surface. Mark unverified requirements as open gaps.
