# Operations Objects

One job: route a change touching monorepo operational tooling to the smallest canonical tool or procedure source.

## Inputs

- Object index: [../_index.md](../_index.md)
- Canonical tool catalog: [../../../../archwiz/TOOL_INDEX.md](../../../../archwiz/TOOL_INDEX.md)
- Change control: [../governance/change-control.md](../governance/change-control.md)

## Process

1. Read the ArchWiz card for tool-surface work, the Optional Visual Review card for initiated file-backed CCTV artifacts, a proposed visual checkpoint, or a later renderer/publication proposal; the Challenge and Certifications Program card for challenge-entry, deadline, or certification routing; or the Competitive Opportunities card for portfolio, resource-allocation, and provider-economics decisions.
2. Open the cited tool source or its governing procedure before editing.
3. Preserve the distinction between cockpit, forensic, autonomous, verification, knowledge, challenge, economic, and optional visual-review work.

## Available operational objects

| Object | Open when… | Stop at |
|---|---|---|
| [archwiz.md](archwiz.md) | changing the cockpit, forensic, autonomous, verification, or knowledge tool surface | the named tool’s source and probe/test boundary |
| [optional-visual-review.md](optional-visual-review.md) | reviewing initiated CCTV cards, proposing a file-backed stage mirror or human checkpoint, or scoping later publication | the canonical source artifact and the separate renderer/publication gate |
| [challenge-program.md](challenge-program.md) | routing a challenge entry, deadline, or certification question | the program's own records in docs/ops/ and its management tree |
| [competitive-opportunities.md](competitive-opportunities.md) | comparing competitive opportunities, challenge award exposure, resource opportunity cost, repo time investment value, or provider economics | docs/ops/COMPETITIVE-OPPORTUNITIES.md and the relevant domain ledger |
| [self-healing-engine.md](self-healing-engine.md) | tracing a failure signal into an incident, recovery plan, repair PR, or attestation | the she/ package source and its per-module live-execution flags |

## Outputs

- A scoped tool change plan with its direct validation surface.

## Human check

Confirm that the change stays in the requested tool category, retains a direct validation path, and does not turn file-backed CCTV artifacts into an unreviewed renderer, public service, or device requirement.
