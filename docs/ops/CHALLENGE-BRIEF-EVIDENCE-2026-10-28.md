## D3 — Builder.io Agent-Native Hackathon

Sources: https://builder.io/hackathon (crawled 2026-10-28 11:48 UTC) and

### The challenge (verbatim)

> Build an app where people and agents work together.
>
> Use the Agent-Native framework to turn your idea into a working app.
> Give your agent a real job and your users an interface to view, edit,
> and act on its work.
>
> Build with Agent-Native and compete for **$10,000 in prizes.**
>
> Open to developers worldwide. Start building today. No registration
> required.

### What makes a strong entry (verbatim)

> **An agent with a purpose** — Build around a clear task: analyze data,
> automate a workflow, or create something users can work with.
>
> **An interface that puts users in control** — Let users explore
> results, make edits, and guide the agent's next steps.
>
> **A connected experience** — Make the UI and agent work with the same
> data and actions, so users can move between direct interaction and
> agent assistance.

### Framework (verbatim)

> Define a capability once with `defineAction()`. Your React UI, agent,
> and integrations call the same code.

### How to enter (Official Rules, verbatim)

> 1. Build a functional application using Agent-Native.
> 2. Submit original work or meaningful new development completed during
>    the Hackathon, beginning October 27, 2026. Identify pre-existing
>    work, templates, libraries, third-party assets, and what changed
>    during the event.
> 3. Provide a project description, creator or team details, contact
>    information, and an accessible demonstration of the working
>    application.
> 4. Record a public Clips demonstration showing the actual application
>    in use. The recording must be viewable without a login or access
>    request. A public repository or live deployment is encouraged but
>    not required.
> 5. Submit the required information and public Clips link through the
>    project submission form by Monday, November 9, 2026 at 11:59 PM PT,
>    and confirm receipt. Recording a clip alone does not submit an
>    entry.
> 6. Agree to the approved Official Rules and confirm eligibility when
>    submitting an entry.

### Judging (Official Rules, verbatim)

> - **Usefulness and Execution: 40%.** A clear problem and a complete
>   working flow. Does the project deliver a useful outcome, reduce
>   manual effort, and make that outcome usable?
> - **Creativity and Design: 20%.** An imaginative idea or interaction.
>   What makes the experience distinctive and enjoyable to use?
> - **Innovation: 20%.** A thoughtful people-and-agent workflow that
>   improves how something gets done, rather than adding AI without a
>   clear purpose.
> - **Cleverness and Use of Agent-Native: 20%.** Resourceful use of
>   Agent-Native to bring the interface, agent behavior, and application
>   capabilities together.
>
> Judges score each criterion from 0 to 10. Each judge's weighted total
> is the sum of each criterion score multiplied by its percentage
> weight. The mean weighted total across judges determines an entry's
> score, and eligible entries are ranked from highest to lowest.
>
> Best Overall App, Best Productivity App, Most Creative App, and Best
> Use of Agent-Native are recognition categories only. They do not
> create additional cash prizes or change the weighted scoring rubric.

## D1 — Strands SDK: Workshop to Ship (AWS Builder Center)

Source: https://builder.aws.com/build/hackathons/8311dcb5-1ece-3d62-a441-2e1c2ad90b86/strands-sdk-workshop-to-ship
(crawled 2026-10-28 14:53 UTC).

### About (verbatim)

> Take the workshop, Building AI Agents with Strands SDK: From Simple
> Loops to Multi-Agent Swarms, then use what you learned to build and
> ship your own AI agent for social good with the Strands Agents SDK.
> Your project should deliver measurable improvements for underserved
> populations across one of three focus areas:
>
> - **Education:** a tutor that turns a textbook chapter into a guided
>   practice set; a mock-interview coach that gives feedback in the
>   user's first language; a tool that generates leveled reading
>   passages for a mixed-level class.
> - **Health:** a daily mental health check-in that tracks mood and
>   surfaces a crisis hotline when it detects risk; an accessibility
>   agent that describes surroundings and reads text aloud for
>   low-vision users; a medication agent that explains a prescription in
>   plain language and flags drug interactions.
> - **Climate resilience:** a heat-wave and drought early warning for
>   smallholder farmers; a router to the nearest cooling center or
>   shelter during extreme weather; a flood-risk guide that walks a
>   household through how to prepare.
>
> Build something that could benefit people or the planet, for a chance
> to win $5,000 in AWS Promotional credits and an AWS Builder swag
> bundle.

### Who can enter (verbatim)

> Open to builders 18 or older with a Builder Center profile. See Rules
> for country and employee exclusions.

### How to qualify (verbatim)

> Complete the free Strands SDK workshop on Builder Center during the
> hackathon window (October 28 to November 4). Workshop completion is
> required to qualify.
>
> 1. Go to the listing of free sandbox workshops on Builder Center.
>    1. Find the workshop, Building AI Agents with Strands SDK: From
>       Simple Loops to Multi-Agent Swarms.
>    2. Click request free sandbox environment.
>    3. Complete the workshop.
> 2. Your project submission must include:
>    1. An agent built with the Strands Agents SDK that addresses a
>       real-world social good challenge.
>    2. A project article on Builder Center describing the agent, what
>       it does, and the social good problem it addresses.
>    3. A link to a public repository with the agent's source code.
>    4. A short, publicly available demo video, under three minutes,
>       showing the agent working.
>    5. An original agent that has not been published before.

### How submissions are judged (verbatim)

> Judging runs in two rounds:
>
> - **Gate 1, AI and human scoring:** every qualifying submission is
>   scored on the rubric below. The top 50 submissions advance.
> - **Gate 2, human judging:** a panel of AWS experts reviews the
>   shortlist and selects the winners.
>
> The rubric:
>
> - **Social impact (25%):** how clearly the project delivers measurable
>   improvement for underserved populations, and the depth of potential
>   benefit for people or the planet.
> - **Technical execution (25%):** how well the agent is built with the
>   Strands Agents SDK and how well it works.
> - **Communication quality (25%):** how clearly the project article and
>   video explain the agent, the problem, and what you learned, in a way
>   that makes the reader or viewer want to try it.
> - **Originality (25%):** the originality of the agent architecture,
>   novel use of AWS services, and how far the project extends beyond
>   the lessons in the workshop.

### Prizes + timeline (verbatim)

> Three winning projects will be selected. Each receives $5,000 in AWS
> Promotional Credits and one AWS Builder swag bundle worth about $600
> (jacket, backpack, keyboard). Each winning participant also receives
> an official winner's certificate. Total prize pool: $16,800.
>

> | --- | --- |
> | Launch | October 28 |
> | Projects due by | November 4 |
> | Gate 1 (AI and human scoring) | Week of November 9 |
> | Gate 2 (human judging) | Week of November 16 |
> | Winners Announced | Week of November 23 |
