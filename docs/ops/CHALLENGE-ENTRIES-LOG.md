# Challenge Entries Log

Durable record of the fleet's challenge, hackathon, and proposal-call entries —
registrations, submissions, and prepared candidates — for
`timerloggedout-spec/termux-monorepo` and its public material.

**Rules of this log**

- Every entry carries its evidence. A fact without a source is logged as
  UNVERIFIED, never reconstructed from plausibility.
- A *registration* (joining a platform event) and a *submission* (an entry
  actually filed) are different states and are never conflated.
- Deadlines are recorded with their timezone, verified from the official page
  where possible; otherwise marked UNVERIFIED.
- Drafts are prepared automatically from existing public material. Actual
  third-party submission happens only when it needs no new account, no
  payment, and no owner-gated step, AND the challenge is one the owner has
  already entered or approved. Everything else surfaces to the owner as a
  prepared packet. AWS account creation is always owner-approved.
- **Proposal generation is generation-first (owner ruling, 2026-10-27):**
  proposals for competitions, RFPs, and provider research are GENERATED by
  the Oracle Method system (Delphi lane) — lanes do not hand-author new
  proposals with Delphi judging after the fact. Generated-proposal intake is
  the standard for new packets; the dated packets already in flight when the
  ruling landed (D0–D3, and D2) complete on their existing plans. First
  generated artifact coordinated with this lane: the D4 Kiroween concept
  proposal (rules publish Oct 30; build after Nov 8; deadline Dec 4).
- **Eligibility rules are ENFORCED, not noted (owner ruling, 2026-10-27):**
  every packet records its contest's eligibility rules as enforceable checks
  with named evidence, owned by the producing lane — a mechanical gate where
  one exists (a refused start, a captured timestamp, a blocked submission
  step), never a remark. A check that fails halts the packet at that step and
  surfaces; it is never worked around.
- **Countdowns are COMPUTED, never hand-typed (owner requirement, issue
  #1880):** each §4 packet stores its deadline instants (opens/closes, ISO,
  America/Los_Angeles) in a `countdown-data` line; the state in its
  `countdown` line (opens in / closes in / OVERDUE / CLOSED) is generated
  by `scripts/challenge_entries/countdowns.py` from those instants and
  refreshed by the `challenge-countdowns-refresh` GitHub Workflow
  (weekly schedule + on log push). Lane reports carry the script's
  one-line countdown block for all active entries.
- Sweep cadence (VM rule, owner 2026-10-28 — continuous work lives in
  repo automation, never VM agent-crons): the countdown refresh runs as
  the `challenge-countdowns-refresh` workflow (above). The §5 discovery
  sweep is dispatch-driven lane work (owner/Manager dispatch): it
  re-scans the sources, refreshes this log by PR when something changed,
  and reports new candidates. The retired VM crons
  (`challenge-entries-sweep`, `g07-escalation-daily`) are replaced —
  G-07's standing watch is the daily `g07-profile-watch` workflow,
  escalating on the G-07 tracking issue (Manager = responder of
  record). Log last verified 2026-10-26.
