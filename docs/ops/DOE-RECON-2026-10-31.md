## Design-ready conclusions

1. **Arm supply is the binding constraint.** Jules is the only
   proven greenfield build arm today ⇒ a competing-build
   campaign needs a second arm un-parked (Gemini via F1) or a
   non-build contrast (command-dispatch as review arm).
2. **The November wave is ready-made live material.** D2/D4/M2/M3
   are already-planned Jules dispatches; a designed factor
   (e.g. dispatch framing: DO-pattern A vs B+A) can ride them at
   zero extra quota, under the mandatory stamps — designed where
   deliberate, observational elsewhere, never retro-dressed.
3. **First competing-build task, when arm B exists:**
   ChronoMancer record emission — bounded, deterministic
   acceptance (records validate vs EXPERIMENT.schema.json;
   fork/continuation pairing; byte-stable re-run), closes a
   named gap either way; loser culled with record, winner's
   approach cherry-picked as the template (owner definition:
   rnd ∘ culling ∘ cherry-pick propagation).
4. **Controls stay minimal** (owner doctrine): pilot-1 + lab-1
   are the control anchor; lab-1 execution is owed but ranks
   below live campaigns.
