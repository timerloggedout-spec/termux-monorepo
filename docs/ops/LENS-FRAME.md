# Lens Frame — Registered Lens Doctrines

Lenses are the family's screening/evaluation frames: a lens
says what readings an entity must carry before a filing
about it is well-formed. Doctrines register here; the
register of record is `docs/ops/DOE-FAMILY.md` (Method
versions). A filing that violates a registered lens
doctrine is MALFORMED — returned, not scored as-is.

## SWOT duality in competitive frames (owner ruling, 2026-11-03)

In a COMPETITIVE frame (hackathons are the type case) the
SWOT quadrants are NOT disjoint: Threats and Opportunities
can be the same target.

- **Dual valence is MANDATORY.** Every screened entity
  carries both readings simultaneously: a rival is, at the
  same time, a recruit/assimilation candidate. A screening
  that records only one valence has not finished.
- **The lens models the CONVERSION MOTION** between the
  readings, not just the two static cells:
  - the threat reading's work = competitive OSINT on the
    entity;
  - the opportunity reading's work = potential-involvement
    recruiting outreach (marketing · BORG tech search).
  A lens output names both motions or states why one has
  not started.
- **Recruitment clause** [owner verbatim bracket]:
  recruitment ≠ %-pay → canBconcept assimilate. Recruitment
  is NOT percentage-of-pay brokerage; in the BORG concept
  it is assimilation into the collective. Any lens output
  pricing recruitment as a pay percentage misreads the
  doctrine.
- **Malformedness.** A SWOT filing that forces a
  single-valence classification in a competitive frame is
  malformed under this doctrine.
- **Scope.** The mandate binds COMPETITIVE frames. Outside
  them, disjoint quadrants remain lawful; the duality is
  available, not forced.

## TAK — Taktikal Awareness Kit (lens registered 2026-11-03)

**Referent: T.A.K. = Taktikal Awareness Kit — OURS.**
Naming RESOLVED on the owner's word (2026-11-03): "Ours
with a 'k' yes." The K-spelling is the fleet coinage and
the distinguishing mark; the C-spelling (Tactical
Awareness Kit) belongs to the DoD namesake. Record: both
namings preserved — the owner's initiative naming
(2026-10-23, claim c4e320344c9a) and the interim
C-identification (2026-11-03, superseded) — relation now
RESOLVED, no longer NOT_ESTABLISHED: same TAK shape, two
owners, spelling carries which.

**The lens.** T.A.K. is a shared tactical-awareness /
common-operating-picture lens: entities, tracks, and
events are held on ONE live picture — position + time +
state — and read for AWARENESS rather than scored
per-item. A T.A.K. reading answers "what does the picture
show now, and what is moving?" — it does not award,
rank, or settle anything by itself; scoring lenses
consume what the picture holds.

**Candidate instances (fleet picture surfaces).** The
fleet already runs picture surfaces that are candidate
instantiations of this lens: the dispatch-tree pages
(lane/dispatch state as a live tree) · the tracking
pages (deadlines, commitments, entity states over time)
· the telemetry pages (provenance/attribution state as
observed). Instantiation status: CANDIDATE — named here
so a T.A.K. filing can cite them; formal binding of any
surface to this lens is a separate recorded act, not
assumed by this note.

**Scope seed #1 (owner-input via the lens trigger,
2026-11-03; scope remains INTAKE — seeds, not a filled
doctrine).**
- Reference domains: the DoD TAK / procurement domains,
  used FOR PROCUREMENT CONCEPTS (their structures inform
  what an entity signal looks like; the namesake's
  contents are not imported as our doctrine).
- Entity-signal sources: SAM.gov procurement and other
  procurement concepts — registrations, awards, and
  entity records as signal sources for the picture.
- Named detection function: **actor network emergence
  signals detection** — new entities, registrations, and
  relationships surfacing in procurement + registration
  data register as EMERGENCE SIGNALS on the picture.

**Composition with SWOT duality.** The picture is where
dual-valence targets get TRACKED. A competitive-frame
entity on the picture carries both readings on the same
track (threat reading ∧ opportunity reading, per the
duality doctrine) — the picture's job is to keep both
visible simultaneously; the conversion motion (rival →
assimilation candidate) is a STATE CHANGE ON THE TRACK,
dated and evidenced like any other track event, never a
silent reclassification. A T.A.K. view that shows only
one valence for a competitive-frame entity is an
incomplete picture, in the same way a single-valence
SWOT filing is malformed.
- **Emergence → SWOT feed.** Emergence detection is
  where T.A.K. feeds SWOT: a newly emerged actor network
  enters the picture UNVALENCED — no reading is assumed
  at entry — and the dual-valence reading is then
  assigned FROM EVIDENCE. Unvalenced-at-entry is the
  honest initial state; valence is an evidence product,
  not a default.
