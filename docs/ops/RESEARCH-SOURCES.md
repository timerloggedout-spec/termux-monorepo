# Research Sources Registry

**Registry:** `ops/delphi/research-sources.json` (schema
`delphi.research-sources.v1`).

External sources registered for Delphi/DoE research cycles. A
source is a **citation route, not a quota surface**: sources are
not scored by the Delphi gate, do not belong in
`ops/delphi/surfaces.json`, and are not research silos
(`ops/delphi/research-silos.json`). Content drawn from a source is
evidence to be cited per item, never pre-trusted.
