## Is “11th Man” the right term?

**Yes, with a definition.** **11th Man** = designated **outsider / red-team challenger** who is *not* the driver — enters to disrupt groupthink. Synonyms: devil’s advocate, red team, opposition researcher. Not “eleventh reviewer in a queue.”

| Role | Duty | When required |
|------|------|----------------|
| **Skeptic** | Attack assumptions, demand falsifiers, question metrics | P1+ claims, matrix score changes, new workflow contracts |
| **Critic** | Attack design/quality of the proposed diff or process | P1+ PRs before merge disposition 🟢 |
| **11th Man** | Outsider challenge — alternate strategy, gap findings, reject-with-evidence when convergence is too fast | P0, security, session/auth, ops workflow merges |
