## Implementation constraints

ATF-04, ATF-05, and ATF-08 are implementation items. They require this proposal to be accepted, a clean integration base, a separate worktree, focused tests, and both repository gates. ATF-03, ATF-09, and ATF-10 are blocking disposition work; they must be resolved before presenting a roster change as merge-ready.

The reverse-engineering, red-team, and combined sensitive-research labels are retained as research signals and are routed through the charter's **Mobile Analysis & Forensics** and **Security Research / Controlled Lab Research** lanes. ATF-11 through ATF-13 establish the label map, target register, controlled-lab evidence model, game-player fleet, and wallet-research architecture; they do not silently erase any existing label or predefine every future specialist role. Operational implementation remains subject to the project-owned target register, task-card evidence, independent review, and the separate approval items above.
