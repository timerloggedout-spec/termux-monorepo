## Principles

0. **Glyph cap (owner correction, 2026-11-04 19:38 PT).** v2
   over-assigned glyphs: only the highest-frequency concepts
   earn a glyph; everything else = chord codes / words. The
   demotions are applied in the map below (📸 → `snp`;
   ⚖✓ → ⚖ + `dch` composition).

1. **One glyph per recurring concept.** A concept that recurs gets
   exactly one glyph; a glyph never carries two concepts in the
   same register.
2. **Chord codes for domain terms.** Where no glyph exists, a
   short chord (letters + a symbol) names the domain term
   (e.g. `N∅`).
3. **Vowel-drop glue only.** Connective glue is restricted to the
   closed set {`bc` · `w/` · `w/o` · `wrt`}; no free abbreviation.
4. **Owner's personal emoji swaps override defaults.** Where the
   owner swaps a glyph, the owner's glyph is the notation.
5. **Accuracy outranks compression** (owner, 2026-11-04 19:39 PT).
   An ambiguous ∨ misleading chord = rejected, however short.
   When a reading could mislead, the notation expands — length
   is never a defense of a wrong reading.

## Shorthand Domains (owner addendum, 2026-11-04 19:36 PT)

The matrice is structured by **Shorthand Domains** — the
steno-domain pattern (medical / legal / journalism each keep
their own chord set). A chord resolves **inside its domain**;
text that crosses domains prefixes the domain when a chord would
be ambiguous out of context. **Core glyphs (the v2 map above)
stay domain-invariant** — they mean the same in every domain.

Collision rulings of record: marketing campaign = `cpn`, never
`cmp` (CMP-1…CMP-6 are DoE campaign-register entries); repo
branch = `brnch`, never `brn` (quota burn); Book position `pos`
is Book-domain only.
