# ELI5 — doe-roles-map.mmd

**What the diagram shows:** two groups of boxes. On the left, the
eight official job hats of the program. On the right, the six
areas of work from this period. An arrow means "this hat is worn
in that area of work."

**What it means, in plain words:**

- The same hat shows up in several areas. The builder hat, for
  example, is worn in four of the six areas — it is the same job
  (making the machinery), just applied to different work.
- Some hats are rare: the gambler hat (taking scored positions)
  appears only in the pool program, and the scout hat (picking
  what enters the next round) only in the D1 work.
- One arrow is dashed because it is not a hat assignment: the
  dispatch desk *reads* the pool program's records when it sends
  out work. It is a handoff between areas, not a job.
- Two extra boxes are warnings, not jobs: two hat names are
  officially reserved but nobody wore them this period, and
  "forecast" is a thing the work produces — never a hat.

**What the diagram does NOT show:** what each hat actually does
day to day, or the proof that the work happened. Those one-line
job descriptions and their receipts live in the register this
picture was drawn from.
