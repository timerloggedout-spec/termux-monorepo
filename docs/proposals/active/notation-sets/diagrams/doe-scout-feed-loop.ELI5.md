# ELI5 — doe-scout-feed-loop.mmd

**What the diagram shows:** a circle. A list of available
players and teams feeds a picker; the picker's choices get
written down as experiments; the desk sends the chosen ones out
to work; and what happens gets written down and flows back into
the list. Then the circle goes around again.

**What it means, in plain words:**

- Nobody gets picked from an old reputation list. The picker
  ("Scout") chooses from the live list, and only from entries
  that pass the current health checks.
- Every pick is recorded as an experiment at the moment it is
  made — so later, the pick itself can be graded, not just the
  work that followed.
- The desk that sends work out reads the same list at the moment
  of sending. Picker, sender, and record-keeper are three
  different duties and they do not blur into each other.
- Results flow back into the list, so the next pick is made with
  fresher information than the last one.

**What the diagram does NOT show:** the health checks
themselves, how talent is evaluated, or the rule that a team is
judged on its own record — never by adding up its members. Those
rules are written in the family and roles documents this loop
was drawn from.
