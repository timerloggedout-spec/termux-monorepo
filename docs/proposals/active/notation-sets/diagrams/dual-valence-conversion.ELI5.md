# ELI5 — dual-valence-conversion.mmd

**What the diagram shows:** one box holding two ways of looking at
the same entity — as a threat, and as an opportunity — with a dashed
arrow for the move that shifts which way is in front.

**What it means, in plain words:**

- The entity does not change. Only our relationship to it changes.
  That is why the arrow is dashed and says the "object part" stays
  exactly the same.
- Both readings stay true the whole time. After the move, the
  opportunity reading is the working one, but the threat reading is
  kept in the record — never deleted.
- The lower box shows the standard move ("assimilation") as two
  steps in a row: first learn everything about them (OSINT), then
  reach out. Steps can be chained; the order of chaining does not
  change the result of the chain.
- The note is a rule, not a decoration: whenever this move is
  recorded, we must also write down what the threat view knew that
  the opportunity view leaves out. If that part is missing, the
  record is incomplete.
