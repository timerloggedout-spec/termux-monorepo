## Q1 — Separation principle: verdict + failure modes

**Verdict: sound as doctrine.** Our marketing states only our
verified record; partner claims live attributed — the evidence
supports it three ways: (i) the fleet's verified record is
sufficient copy (one surface already passes a claims gate on
exactly that basis, honest negatives included — `d0:claims-gate`);
(ii) every borrowed-claim instance in evidence is also an
unverifiable one (benchmark superlative, partner capability
pitch); (iii) the attributed-card pattern proves attribution
works where it is applied (`landing:cellcog-card`). The blend's
defense reduced to continuity + a footer disclosure.

**Failure modes (both surviving variants, honestly):**
1. *Boundary creep* (tiered's risk, strict's raison d'être):
   the 2026-10-27 ruling already confined partner claims to the
   grid card — and the hero never followed. Any doctrine without
   a mechanical check (claims gate on copy changes) decays at
   exactly this seam.
2. *Attribution rot* (both): surfaces drift from the SSOT —
   the grid today omits two active lanes PARTNERS.md lists; a
   README link (Runable) exists in no registry. Doctrine must
   ship with the feed/check, not just the rule.
3. *Label fatigue* (tiered): a labeled superlative still borrows
   credibility by placement; readers skim labels.
4. *Marker debt* (tiered): NOT ESTABLISHED offers (Polsia,
   Neon) can sit unverified indefinitely; strictness forces
   resolution, tiering tolerates the debt. (Mitigation on
   record: time-boxed markers that expire at window closes.)
5. *Verification bottleneck* (strict): claims without a receipt
   path go unstated — honest blanks read thinner than
   competitors' borrowed superlatives. This is a cost, priced
   in, not a refutation.
6. *Participation confusion* (strict, if misread): separation
   governs CLAIMS, not participation — referral lanes are a
   real replenishment surface (owner standing rule #574) and
   stay; what ends is speaking in a partner's voice.

## Q3 — Claims discipline (the spec the panel outcome supports)

**Fleet voice may assert (each with its receipt path):**
merge-verified landings (PR + merge SHA) · receipt-verified
runs (demo receipts, pass outcomes, reproduced counts) · live
feed values with timestamps · participation facts (a lane is
live, a link is tracked) · service relations with evidence
(a partner runs a review lane here — check-run records) ·
honest negatives (submissions=0, redemptions=0, NOT
ESTABLISHED markers).

**Fleet voice must never borrow:** partner benchmarks or
rankings as fleet claims (e.g. "#1 in Deep Research") ·
partner product capabilities as our features · unverified
offer amounts (Polsia 500 credits; Manus 500 third-party
reported) · superlatives generally · conversion or popularity
claims without records · pipeline partners presented as
active.

**Partner claims may live:** on the partner's own surfaces;
on fleet surfaces only attributed + labeled (tiered) or not
at all (strict) — the owner chooses the tier; hero/brand is
fleet-record-only under both.
