# Daily Five-Item Briefing Contract

The briefing is a daily research product, not a generic news roundup.

## Required categories
AI & technology; science & research; open-source/developer tools; emerging technologies; FOSS-driven research/procurement/concepts.

## Selection
Prioritize breaking developments, deep research, practical tools, durable stories, monorepo relevance, and vendor-lock-in reduction.

## Item contract
Every item must include:
- concise headline
- H0/H1/H2/H3 horizon
- evidence status
- summary
- why it matters
- practical opportunity/procurement implication
- Termux-monorepo relevance when applicable
- primary/authoritative sources
- confidence
- unresolved questions when material
- relevant image accompanying the headline

## Source hierarchy
1. Primary project repositories and official releases
2. Official technical documentation
3. Peer-reviewed papers and preprints
4. Standards bodies
5. Official security advisories
6. Government/public research
7. Reproducible benchmarks
8. First-party provider catalogs
9. Secondary reporting for discovery/context

## Radar Watch
H2/H3 material that does not justify one of the five main items goes into a short Radar Watch section.

## No padding
If a category has no material development, omit it rather than filling the briefing with low-value news.
