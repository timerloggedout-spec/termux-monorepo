- `MANIFEST.json` — the wiring: every entry names its experiment, its proof (artifact citation + provenance, oracle run, verdict), and the DATA LAYERS the proof proves over (named refs: repo paths · QUE measurement plane · lane-events/Neon · telemetry corpora).
- Proof artifacts live in the Lean home, `research/nse-023/examples/`, and are cited here — never silently moved.

Rule (owner, 2026-11-03): an entry missing its proof OR its data-layer refs reads **INCOMPLETE**, as does any experiment of record with no entry.
