## Framing (binding)

This index is a **walkable-today competence path**: every entry below is open and free *now*, verified firsthand on 2026-11-03, and together they cover the material the gated rung examines. It is **learning input, ¬ evidence** — walking this path produces no completion record, no matrix state change, and **∅ certificate claims**. The rung itself (`intake-sfi-abm`, *Introduction to Agent-Based Modeling*) stays **GATED** in the [Value Matrix](/guides/certifications-value-matrix) until its funding/session gates clear; nothing here re-opens that verdict.

## The path (suggested walk order)

1. **Watch the lectures** (the course's own video units, in course order) — builds the ① syllabus spine: what ABM is, building/extending models, components, analysis, verification/validation/replication, classic models, advanced ABM.
2. **Run the models** in NetLogo alongside each unit — ① is a build-a-model course; the Models Library holds the canonical models the lectures dissect (Schelling, El Farol, flocking, Wolf-Sheep).
3. **Build Sugarscape with Traders in Mesa** (Python) — the same modeling discipline in the fleet's own stack: agents, landscapes, trade rules, data collection, batch runs. This is the transfer step from watching to doing.
