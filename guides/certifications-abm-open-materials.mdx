## Framing (binding)

This index is a **walkable-today competence path**: every entry below is open and free *now*, verified firsthand on 2026-11-03, and together they cover the material the gated rung examines. It is **learning input, ¬ evidence** — walking this path produces no completion record, no matrix state change, and **∅ certificate claims**. The rung itself (`intake-sfi-abm`, *Introduction to Agent-Based Modeling*) stays **GATED** in the [Value Matrix](/guides/certifications-value-matrix) until its funding/session gates clear; nothing here re-opens that verdict.

## Method — open-equivalency builds (standing ladder method)

This index is **instance #1** of a standing ladder method (owner-named type, 2026-11-03), applicable to any gated credential or curriculum:

1. **Decompose** the target's requirements from the syllabus of record.
2. **Map** each requirement → open materials with resolvable sources.
3. **GAP REGISTER** — requirements with no open equivalent are NAMED, never smoothed over (proctored exams · accredited assessment · issuer stamps are the usual irreducible gaps).
4. **VERIFICATION LEG** — executed artifacts from the open materials (their quizzes/projects, run + receipted) stand as competence evidence.
5. **CLAIM BOUNDARY** — equivalence of LEARNING is what gets proven; equivalence of CREDENTIAL is never claimed (the issuer stamp is the issuer's; our evidence form is the derived {skill, KB} package).

Exemplars of the pattern: the OSSU degree-equivalent CS curriculum · Teach Yourself CS · the MIT Challenge pattern. For this instance: the gated course (`intake-sfi-abm`) is the target of record; this index is its legs ②–③; the registers below complete legs ①, ③ and ④.

**Standard of record:** [docs/ops/EQUIVALENCE-PROOF.md](../docs/ops/EQUIVALENCE-PROOF.md) (DoE equivalence-proof standard v0, PR #4342) — this note *cites* that standard; no parallel standard is restated here. Under it, a build is **PROVEN** ⇔ (a) the syllabus of record comes from the issuer source, ¬ a summary (b) the mapping is total — every requirement → a resolvable open source ∨ a gap-register entry by name (c) executed verification with receipts (d) the boundary is stated. Short of that, the build is **CLAIM-grade**, stamped per that doc. Coverage is reported **per requirement class, never as a blended %** (K9).

### Leg ① — target decomposition (syllabus of record, 9 units)

What is ABM and why use it · building a simple model · extending models · creating agent-based models · the components of an ABM · analyzing ABMs · verification, validation, and replication · history of ABM and classic models · advanced ABM. Plus the course's operating requirements: build models from the ground up in NetLogo, analyze results, and pass a graded exam per module (in-session).

### Leg ③ — GAP REGISTER (no open equivalent — named, not smoothed)

| Requirement of record | Open equivalent | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Graded exam per module (in-session, passing marks) | Post-close quizzes (self-assessment only, ungraded) | **GAP — irreducible**: no open route reproduces issuer-graded assessment |

### Leg ④ — VERIFICATION LEG (what counts as competence evidence here)

Executed artifacts from the open materials, run + receipted: the post-close course quizzes worked through · the NetLogo library models opened, run and modified against each unit · the Mesa Sugarscape-with-Traders build completed in Colab (or against the maintained `mesa/mesa` example) with its data-collection and batch-run outputs. Receipts live with the work (fleet capture discipline); they evidence *competence*, and are the raw material for the derived {skill, KB} package — they are not, and never become, the credential.

## The path (suggested walk order)

1. **Watch the lectures** (the course's own video units, in course order) — builds the ① syllabus spine: what ABM is, building/extending models, components, analysis, verification/validation/replication, classic models, advanced ABM.
2. **Run the models** in NetLogo alongside each unit — ① is a build-a-model course; the Models Library holds the canonical models the lectures dissect (Schelling, El Farol, flocking, Wolf-Sheep).
3. **Build Sugarscape with Traders in Mesa** (Python) — the same modeling discipline in the fleet's own stack: agents, landscapes, trade rules, data collection, batch runs. This is the transfer step from watching to doing.

## What this path does not do

- It does **not** enrol anyone, earn the SFI certificate, or satisfy any part of the rung's completion rule — if the rung ever clears its gates, the in-session course (with graded exams) is still the earning route.
- It does **not** change any matrix cell: `intake-sfi-abm` remains INTAKE · GATED with `evidence_requirement` [`issuer-page`, `cost-feasibility`].
- Working through it produces *capability* — the derived-artifact step (an ABM skill + KB entry, monorepo-first, earner `termux-monorepo`) belongs to the completion package noted in the intake record, and is authored from real work if/when the tree takes the path up — never pre-claimed here.
