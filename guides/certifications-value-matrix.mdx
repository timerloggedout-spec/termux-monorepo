## Rules of This Matrix (DoE conformity — binding)

- Every cell is an **expectation record**, not a bare number: it carries a value or range **and** its basis — `measured` (telemetry/capture of record), `issuer` (platform-published rule), `assumption` (labelled, re-priced at first instance), or **INTAKE/HELD** (unevidenced — no value claimed).
- The ALE view and this matrix are **derived views of the same numbers**. No cell is computed twice from different inputs.
- **Unevidenced cells carry INTAKE/HELD. There are no dressed-up zeros in this document.** A `0` appears only where zero is itself the evidenced value (e.g. completions counted on the record).
- Earner = `termux-monorepo` ∀ rows. The certifications tree manages (oversight, enrolment, evidence); it does not earn. Completion ⇔ fleet-automation run ∧ telemetry-evidenced ∧ per-model attribution on record (`docs/ops/CERTIFICATIONS-PROGRAM.md`, PR #3249).
- Dates columns (wall + buffer-landing) and evidence-state column are supplied by the Roadmap lane and reconciled into this single matrix; ladder is rows-of-record owner.
- Buffer rule (owner, 2026-11-02): dated items land −1d ahead of wall; landing dates govern.

## INTAKE — Certifications as a Category (not yet enrolled)

The matrix covers **Certifications as a category**, not only the enrolled ladder. Every cert family/provider of record across the L1–L6 categories appears below as an **INTAKE row** until it is enrolled. INTAKE rows carry **no estimated values** — each names **the gap**: the specific evidence that would price the row and promote it to a full expectation record above.

Two reads are served: (1) sequencing the active track (matrix above), and (2) prioritizing what enters it. An INTAKE row is promoted when its gap evidence lands and a Role Requirements Plan (plan-before-spend) is opened for it; promotion order = competition linkage first, then deadline, then target-role gap fit, then cost class.

## Row notes (provenance)

- **FCC L1 time/quota** derive from the [Evidence Index](/guides/certifications-evidence-index) L1 window section (measured T rate; labelled per-type assumptions) and the fleet-logs capture PRs — the same numbers the ALE view reads. Re-price checkpoints: unit 011 (first W) and unit 025 (first P).
- **AMD Academy rows**: the League gate is set-level (≥5 courses ⇒ +500 pts ∧ podium eligibility $2,500 / $1,500 / $1,000). Plan of record names C1, C2, C3, C4, C7 with walls; C5 exists in the roadmap skeleton windows (≤11-20) but carries no landing date in the 2026-11-02 landing record, and C6 carries neither — both ship HELD rather than inherit a neighbour's date.
- **ROCm retake policy** is the single largest unpriced exposure in T1: if the exam allows no in-window retake, the +500 is single-shot. HELD until the issuer rule is on record.
- **L2–L6** are sequenced L1≫L2≫…≫L6, plan-before-spend; every quantified cell is HELD until the rung's Role Requirements Plan prices it. That is the DoE-correct state, not missing work.
- **Prize-received class** carries ∅ work targets by the HYBRID ruling: deadline/prize certs stay with their owning lanes; this matrix tracks value and state only.
- **Reconciliation (2026-11-02)**: dates + evidence-state columns folded from the Roadmap lane's per-row record of the same date — all walls/landings match the landing-dates record; C5/C6 appear in **neither** lane's dates record, so their HELD state is a two-lane fact, not a single-lane gap. INTAKE-row dates/evidence follow the same pattern (NOT_ESTABLISHED / ∅), confirmation requested from Roadmap.
