## Capability synthesis

Use My-Jogyo for the research lifecycle and role separation. Use Docxology for reproducible pipeline discipline. Our implementation combines both with canonical evidence, immutable run/SHA linkage, independent validation, and repository-owned promotion gates.

These are comparative references: benchmark and extract patterns, then implement repository-owned components with explicit provenance.

Metadata-first. Live pins under smods only when dual-gate and task outcome are verified. Provider references live in categories 05 and 11; they remain separate from the 12 research-template silos.
