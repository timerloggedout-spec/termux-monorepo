# Delphic Observatory glossary

- **Evidence field:** source artefacts and receipts arranged across graph, matrix and tensor projections.
- **Decision window:** bounded period where evidence, uncertainty and decision criteria are evaluated together.
- **Research lane:** independently owned research workspace publishing normalized evidence to the management hub.
- **H0–H3:** horizon-signal classification used for foresight prioritization.
- **DAG:** directed acyclic graph used for dependency and provenance relationships.
- **Trellis:** layered state-transition graph that can preserve multiple paths and recombination.
- **Markov path:** state sequence modeled through transition probabilities.
- **Provenance:** source identity, version, timestamp and evidence lineage.
- **Feed freshness:** elapsed time since a source observation or update.
- **Configured model instance:** provider, model/version, endpoint, protocol, account scope, prompt/tool manifests, role, moniker, context and instance key.
- **Read-only projection:** presentation layer with observation access and zero mutation authority.
- **Siglary:** concise notation operators for dense, precise operational communication.
- **DO statement:** affirmative action, state, owner or gate phrased as a direct instruction or fact.
