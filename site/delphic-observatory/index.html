Platform responsibilities

GitHub: source of truth, lane registry, schemas, PRs, issues, Actions receipts and release provenance.

Vercel: public UI, branch previews, static assets and presentation deployment.

Render: planned API/cache and scheduled feed aggregation; no legacy service is repurposed without a dedicated health and ownership contract.

Safety and authority boundaries

Read-only projection. No task dispatch, quota consumption, paid fallback, participant provisioning, wallet mutation, culling, policy override or evidence promotion. Termux Origin remains reported offline until independently verified online.

Wolfram supports symbolic derivation and review only; runtime calculations remain deterministic and local.

Feed status and provenance

The browser polls public GitHub endpoints. Render aggregation is opt-in after its service is provisioned and health-checked.
